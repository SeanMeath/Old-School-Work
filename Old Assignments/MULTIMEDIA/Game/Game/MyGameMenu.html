FRIEND SIMULATOR

The one and only game to simulate the interactions between people

Its really just a guessing game

You play as a ghost. Ghosts are spooky. Therefore it's really hard for a ghost to make friends since he ends up just scaring them away. This makes you very sad. You're goal is to make as many friends as possible before the amount of heartbreaks make you too sad to continue, leaving you in a constant state of depression and sadness.

You can move around the plane of existence by either using wasd or the arrow keys. Once you run into a possible friend boxes will appear above you that must be clicked on to choose how you wish to interact with the newcomer. As first encounters are very important if you don't make the cut then the person will leave you thus breaking one of you're 3 hearts :(
